About

Ann Meleney moved to Martha's Vineyard in 2012 and never looked back. The island awakened something in her, drawing her fully into her artistic life and into the heart of its community. Featherstone Center for the Arts became a second home — a place to deepen her practice across mixed media and collage, and to venture into new territory, most recently ceramics. Her work draws from the natural world, from animals and the living landscape around her, but she isn't afraid to turn her eye toward the political — meeting the moment with the same directness she brings to everything else.

Life off the canvas is just as rich. Ann teaches swim to babies and children with special needs, does water aerobics, and hosts the kind of Friday neighborhood dinners people look forward to all week. Summers mean long afternoons on the porch with board games and whoever wanders by. At the center of it all are her two granddaughters, Anya and Ramona, both two years old.
